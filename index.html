Story

I'm Boston born and bred, but currently live in Westchester (NY) with my wife, two daughters, and guard dog. I'm also a New Zealand citizen, and try to spend as much time there as I can.

As the son of two doctors and husband of another, health is a big part of my operating system. Being outside is where I'm happiest — whether it's on the trails, slopes, or golf course.

I've been entrepreneurial since I was a kid. Right now, I'm focused on real estate software.

Tech

I studied math and economics at Wesleyan, and then took a consulting job at EY. Living in hotels and spreadsheets got old...so I quit and dove headfirst into software startups.

Then I bought my first piece of real estate, which kick-started an ever-growing interest in the intersection of physical space and technology. So, I've spent the last 9 years building software startups that make life a little easier for property managers and service providers. Below are some of my recent ventures.

Reva

Co-Founder & Partner (2023 – Present)
Software that helps vacation rental managers leverage guest reviews to drive insights and control their reputation. We're a scrappy team on a mission to help operators turn guest feedback into a competitive advantage.

Mews

Investor (2023 – Present)
Software that helps hotels and hostels automate workflows through AI-powered insights and system integrations. One of the fastest growing tech companies in hospitality, Mews powers over 15K customers across 85 countries.

Breezeway

VP, Growth & Ops (2018 – 2026)
Software that helps vacation rental managers automate property service. Employee #5, and member of mgmt team. Helped find product-market fit, hire 100+ people, and grow ARR by 125X. Acquired by private equity in Feb '26.
